# Acceptable use of personal devices

**Service:** intune
**Type:** policy
**Applies to:** all staff

## What is permitted

You may access corporate **mail and calendar** from a personal phone or
tablet, provided the device:

- has a passcode or biometric lock
- runs a supported operating system version
- is enrolled for mail access through Company Portal

No approval is needed for this.

## What is not permitted

Personal devices must not be used to:

- access shared drives or internal applications
- store corporate documents locally outside the managed mail app
- connect to the corporate VPN

If your role requires any of these from a non-corporate device, that is a
laptop request, not a personal-device question.

## What enrolment does and does not do

Enrolling for mail access lets IT enforce the passcode requirement and
remotely remove **corporate** mail data. It does not give IT access to your
personal photos, messages, or applications, and it does not allow a full
device wipe.

## Leaving the company, or losing the device

Report a lost or stolen personal device holding corporate mail the same day.
Corporate mail data is removed remotely; your personal data is untouched.

On leaving, corporate mail access is revoked and the managed mail data is
removed automatically.
