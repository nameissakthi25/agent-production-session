# Requesting a new or replacement laptop

**Service:** laptop-support
**Type:** policy
**Applies to:** all staff, with manager approval

## Standard refresh cycle

Corporate laptops are replaced every three years. You do not need to request
a refresh — the hardware team raises the ticket and contacts you when your
device is due.

## Requesting outside the cycle

Raise a request if your device is lost, stolen, damaged beyond repair, or no
longer meets the requirements of your role. Include:

- your current device hostname
- the reason for the request
- your manager's name for approval

## What happens next

1. Your manager approves or rejects the request. Nothing moves until they do.
2. The hardware team confirms stock and gives an estimated date. Standard
   models are usually available within five working days; non-standard
   specifications require additional approval and take longer.
3. The device is enrolled in Intune before collection. It will not be handed
   over until it reports compliant — see *Device compliance requirements*.

## Non-standard specifications

Requests above the standard specification need a written justification and
approval from both your manager and the budget holder for your cost centre.
Expect this to add one to two weeks.

## Returning the old device

Return it within five working days of collecting the replacement. Devices are
wiped on return. Copy anything you need to your OneDrive first — nothing is
recoverable after the wipe.
