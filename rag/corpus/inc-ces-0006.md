# Internal Web Service TLS outage

**Service:** load-balancer
**Source incident:** INC-CES-0006
**Priority:** high

## Symptom

Internal staff in the <LOCATION> data center were trying to access the Internal Web Service over HTTPS for the internal-service-api and internal-dashboard endpoints (internal-service.company.local), but browsers showed certificate warnings and requests through the F5 load balancer (lb-prod-east-01.company.local) failed TLS negotiation. Users could not reach the service securely, and monitoring reported TLS handshake failures and 502 responses in us-east-1-dc1 after the service certificate passed its expiration on 2025-12-13 without the renewed chain being deployed. Initially reported by <PERSON> (<EMAIL>) from the Finance team, who was unable to load the internal dashboard from her workstation <HOSTNAME>.

## Diagnosis

Diagnostics confirmed the outage was caused by an expired service certificate (CN=internal-service.company.local) and invalid deployed chain on the F5 load balancer (lb-prod-east-01.company.local) in the <LOCATION> data center. Endpoint checks showed the leaf certificate had expired as of 2025-12-13, chain validation failed from the load balancer/client path (client IP <IP> used for testing), and monitoring controls did not prevent or escalate the impending expiration in time. Service was restored after certificate renewal by <PERSON> and redeployment of the full chain to both the service endpoint and load balancer. PagerDuty alerting was restored to notify the infra-certs-team@company.local distribution list.

## Root cause

The TLS certificate for internal-service.company.local issued by the Internal Certificate Authority expired and the renewed full certificate chain was not deployed to the F5 load balancer, causing TLS handshake rejection and HTTPS outage for the Internal Web Service.

## Resolution

1. Confirmed the presented certificate for internal-service.company.local was expired (Not After: 2025-12-13T00:00:00Z) and identified that the active chain on the load balancer lb-prod-east-01.company.local was no longer valid for client trust. Verification performed by <PERSON> (<USER>) from the <LOCATION> NOC.
2. Requested and obtained a renewed TLS certificate from the Internal Certificate Authority for the affected service endpoint (CN=internal-service.company.local, SAN: internal-service.company.local, internal-dashboard.company.local). CSR generated by <USER>, approved by <PERSON> (<USER>).
3. Installed the renewed certificate and complete certificate chain on the Internal Web Service endpoint (internal-svc-prod-01.company.local) and reloaded the service TLS configuration. Deployment verified by <USER> via openssl s_client handshake test.
4. Updated the F5 load balancer (lb-prod-east-01.company.local) to present the renewed certificate chain on VIP 10.50.1.20:443 and reloaded the affected HTTPS virtual service. Confirmed chain validity from client subnet 10.22.14.0/24 by <PERSON>.
5. Validated successful end-to-end TLS handshakes from client (<HOSTNAME> at <IP>) and monitoring perspectives across regions, and restored certificate expiration alerting to PagerDuty (notifying infra-certs-team@company.local) to prevent recurrence. <PERSON> and <PERSON> confirmed service access restored. Closure approved by <PERSON> (<USER>).
